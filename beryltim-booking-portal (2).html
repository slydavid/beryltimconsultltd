Frequently Asked Questions

Good to know before you book

Answers to the questions we get asked most about our consultation sessions.

What exactly is covered in a consultation session?
Each session is scoped to the package you select. The free Clarity Session offers guidance only, with no written report, document review, or implementation. Paid sessions add personalized advice, written summaries, strategic roadmaps, and, for the Executive package, compliance planning and growth recommendations. We'll confirm scope with you before the session begins.
How and when do I pay for a paid session?
Once you select a paid package on the booking form, our Moniepoint account details appear automatically so you can transfer the session fee right away. After payment, you can upload your receipt on the same form. This isn't required, but it helps our team confirm your slot faster. Your booking is fully secured once payment is received.
Can I reschedule my consultation?
Yes. You may reschedule up to 24 hours before your session at no extra cost by contacting us via WhatsApp, phone, or email using the details in the footer below. Rescheduling requests made after this window will be accommodated based on availability.
What is your cancellation policy?
Free sessions can be cancelled any time with no penalty. For paid sessions, cancellations made at least 24 hours in advance are eligible for a full refund or credit toward a future session. Late cancellations or no-shows may not be eligible for a refund.
Do you offer implementation support after the session?
Implementation support is not included in the Clarity or 30-Minute sessions. Strategy and Executive Strategy sessions include a written roadmap or checklist you can act on independently. If you'd like hands-on implementation support, our team can discuss a separate engagement after your session.
